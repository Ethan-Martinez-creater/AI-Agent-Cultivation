# Research Workflow v1 Reference Basis

This is a design record for the official `official.research@1` Workflow package. The cited material supplies process principles; it does not certify this Workflow or its outputs.

## Deliberate limits

The Workflow stores bounded, versioned research artifacts and validates their structure and provenance. It does not certify source quality, establish that a hypothesis is true, or replace a domain expert's scientific judgement. A completed Workflow means the declared artifacts and review path completed; it does not mean a research conclusion has been finally proven.

This Workflow is not a substitute for a systematic-review protocol or reporting standard, institutional or legal ethics review, informed consent, laboratory or field safety review, biosafety/security approval, or other discipline-specific approvals. Users remain responsible for required review and approvals before collecting data or carrying out real-world experiments. Human Bridge or external work is required when an experiment needs a person, instrument, external system, or approval that the local Tool Runtime cannot safely perform.

No vendor-specific search engine, reference manager, dataset host, laboratory platform, publisher, or submission system is part of the frozen v1 package.
