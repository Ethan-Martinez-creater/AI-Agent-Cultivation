# Preset portraits

The ten numbered PNG portraits are copied from the user-provided `docs/avatar/ChatGPT Image Sep 30, 2026, 07_40_*.png` resources, matched by their `-1` through `-10` suffixes. The original resources are preserved. They are rendered with a circular CSS crop; no remote avatar request or image-generation service runs in the product.

`human-bridge.png` and `user.png` remain independent bundled identity portraits from the official DiceBear 9.x Avataaars API. Avataaars artwork: Pablo Stanley, [original project and license](https://avataaars.com/), free for personal and commercial use. API/style attribution: [DiceBear Avataaars](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/avataaars/).

Dedicated identity acquisition settings: 256×256, `backgroundColor=eaf2ff`, `clothing=blazerAndShirt`, `clothesColor=3c4f5c`, `mouth=smile`, `eyes=default`, `eyebrows=defaultNatural`, `accessoriesProbability=0`, `facialHairProbability=0`. Seeds are `cultivation-human-bridge` and `cultivation-user`, with hair `longButNotTooLong` and `shortRound`. They contain no user data.
