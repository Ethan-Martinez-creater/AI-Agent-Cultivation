# Preset portraits

Bundled, fixed PNG portraits generated through the official DiceBear 9.x Avataaars API. The app never requests remote avatars at runtime.

Avataaars artwork: Pablo Stanley, [original project and license](https://avataaars.com/), free for personal and commercial use. API/style attribution: [DiceBear Avataaars](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/avataaars/).

The ten numbered images use distinct hair silhouettes and calm facial expressions. `human-bridge.png` and `user.png` are dedicated identity portraits. Acquisition settings: 256×256, `backgroundColor=eaf2ff`, `clothing=blazerAndShirt`, `clothesColor=3c4f5c`, `mouth=smile`, `eyes=default`, `eyebrows=defaultNatural`, `accessoriesProbability=0`, `facialHairProbability=0`. Seeds are `cultivation-01` through `cultivation-10`, `cultivation-human-bridge`, and `cultivation-user`; they contain no user data. Hair selections in this order: `shortFlat`, `straight01`, `shortCurly`, `bob`, `shaggy`, `bun`, `shortWaved`, `curly`, `theCaesarAndSidePart`, `straight02`, `longButNotTooLong`, `shortRound`.
